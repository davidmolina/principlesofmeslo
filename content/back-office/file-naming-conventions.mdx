## File Naming

Consistent file naming improves organization and retrieval.

### Example (Invoices)

`[YYYYMMDD] [Company] [Invoice No.] [Type] [Amount]`

Example:

`20221022 Comcast 12345 G&A $306.33`

The `G&A` denotes it's a general and administrative expense.

Then store these receipts onto your server (onsite or cloud) and reduce paper receipts.
