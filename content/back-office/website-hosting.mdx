## Website Hosting

Hosting platforms store and deliver websites to visitors. You will either use a content management system (CMS) or write code.

A CMS is a drag-and-drop platform to update your website, whereas coding will require some work under the hood.

If you don't know how to code, use Codecademy, freeCodeCamp, or Operation Code (veteran-friendly).
