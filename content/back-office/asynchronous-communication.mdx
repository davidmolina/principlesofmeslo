## Asynchronous Communication

Asynchronous communication allows teams to collaborate without real-time meetings.

### Why It Matters

It reduces meeting load, improves documentation, and keeps work moving across jobsite schedules.

### Prompt

What should be asynchronous versus a meeting?

### Key Steps

- Use a written-first tool (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, or Discord).
- Keep updates and decisions in threads so they can be found later.
- Choose a platform with strong API capabilities so you can connect the dots across your 3rd party tools.
