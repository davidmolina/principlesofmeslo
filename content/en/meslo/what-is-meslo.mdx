---
title: What is MESLO
tags: framework, what, is, meslo
---

MESLO is a framework used to structure construction estimating and operational planning.

It stands for:

Material  
Equipment  
Subcontractors  
Labor  
Other Costs  
Insurance/Bond  
Overhead  
Profit  
Taxes

Each component represents a core dimension of project cost, pricing discipline, and operational planning.

The purpose of MESLO is to make estimating more structured, defensible, and operationally aligned.
