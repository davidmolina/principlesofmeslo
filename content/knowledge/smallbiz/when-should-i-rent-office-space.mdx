## When should a small business rent office or retail space?

Rent space when it clearly improves sales, operations, or credibility enough to justify the ongoing overhead.

Model rent, utilities, furniture, and setup costs before signing. Match space decisions to customer behavior and workflow needs. Avoid long leases if demand is still uncertain.
