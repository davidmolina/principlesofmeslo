## How should I choose a payment processor?

Choose a processor based on reliability, fees, payout speed, and fit with how customers pay you. Convenience and clarity matter.

Compare in-person, online, invoice, and recurring payment options. Review fees for cards, transfers, and chargebacks. Make sure it integrates with your bookkeeping or point-of-sale tools.
