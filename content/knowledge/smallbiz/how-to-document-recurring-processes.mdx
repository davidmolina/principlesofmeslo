## Which business processes should I document first?

Document the processes that happen often, cause mistakes, or slow the team down. Start where confusion costs the most.

Prioritize sales, onboarding, invoicing, customer service, and delivery steps. Write the current process first before trying to perfect it. Use checklists so people can follow the process under pressure.
