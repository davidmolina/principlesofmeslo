## Do small businesses need standard operating procedures?

Yes, if you want consistent quality and easier training. SOPs reduce dependence on memory and make growth less chaotic.

Document the most repeated tasks first. Keep each SOP short, specific, and easy to find. Assign an owner to update the SOP when the process changes.
