## How should I pay myself as a business owner?

Pay yourself with a clear method instead of random withdrawals. Consistent owner pay improves planning and protects business cash.

Ask your CPA whether salary, draw, or distributions fit your entity. Separate owner compensation from business expense categories. Do not drain operating cash just because revenue came in this week.
