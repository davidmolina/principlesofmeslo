## Can AI help a small business create marketing content?

Yes, AI can speed up content drafts, but the business still needs clear positioning and human editing. Fast content is useless if it sounds generic.

Use AI to turn customer questions into posts, emails, and FAQs. Edit for local relevance, accuracy, and real examples. Use one brand voice so content still feels consistent.
