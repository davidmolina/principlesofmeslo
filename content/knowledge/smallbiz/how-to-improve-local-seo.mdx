## How can I improve local SEO for my business?

Local SEO improves when your business information is consistent and your website clearly matches local search intent.

Keep your name, address, phone, and hours consistent across listings. Create service pages that mention real locations and customer problems. Earn reviews, local links, and branded searches over time.
