## What credit score do I need for a business loan?

Lenders look at personal credit, business performance, debt, and cash flow together. There is no single universal score cutoff for every loan.

Check both personal credit and business credit before applying. Improve payment history and reduce unnecessary debt if possible. Ask lenders what minimums they use for the specific product.
