## How can I build a simple brand without overthinking it?

A small-business brand starts with consistency, not complexity. Buyers should quickly recognize your name, promise, and style.

Use one clear logo, one short message, and one consistent voice. Match your website, social profiles, signage, and proposals. Keep the brand aligned with the customer you want most.
