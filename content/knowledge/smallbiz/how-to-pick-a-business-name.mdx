## How should I choose a business name?

Choose a name that is clear, easy to say, easy to spell, and available online. A simple name usually beats a clever one.

Check state registration, domain name, and social handle availability. Make sure customers can understand and remember it quickly. Avoid names that limit future growth if you may expand services later.
