## When should I open a business bank account?

Open it as soon as you form the business or start taking money. Mixed personal and business spending makes taxes, bookkeeping, and trust harder.

Use a separate account for income, expenses, and owner pay. Ask about monthly fees, minimum balances, and online tools. Connect the account to bookkeeping software from the start.
