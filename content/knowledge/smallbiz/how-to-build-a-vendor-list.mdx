## How should I manage vendors and suppliers?

A good vendor list improves speed, pricing, and reliability. Supplier relationships are part of operational resilience.

Keep contact info, pricing terms, lead times, and backup options in one place. Review supplier performance on quality and responsiveness. Avoid relying on one vendor when the category is critical.
