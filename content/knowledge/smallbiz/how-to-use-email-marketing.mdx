## Is email marketing still useful for small businesses?

Yes, email works well when it delivers useful reminders, education, and offers to people who already know you. It supports repeat business and referrals.

Collect email addresses with permission from customers and prospects. Send short updates, offers, seasonal reminders, and practical tips. Measure opens, clicks, replies, and conversions over time.
