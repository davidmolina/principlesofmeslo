## How can AI help with customer support?

AI can help answer repeated questions and organize information, but it should not create confusion in sensitive situations.

Use AI for common questions, intake, and internal knowledge lookup. Escalate billing issues, complaints, and exceptions to humans fast. Train the system on your actual policies and answers.
