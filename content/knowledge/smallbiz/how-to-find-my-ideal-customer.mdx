## How do I identify my ideal customer?

Your ideal customer is the buyer with a real problem, the budget to solve it, and a reason to act now. Clarity here improves marketing and sales.

Study your best current customers and look for shared traits. Define industry, location, budget, urgency, and buying behavior. Build messaging around the problem they already feel.
