## What should a basic small-business website include?

A basic website should help people understand your offer and contact you fast. It is a sales tool, not just an online brochure.

Include who you help, what you do, pricing cues, and contact action. Add testimonials, service area, and clear next steps. Make the site fast, mobile friendly, and easy to update.
