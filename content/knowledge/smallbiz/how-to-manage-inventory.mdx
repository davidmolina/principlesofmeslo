## How can a small business manage inventory better?

Good inventory management protects cash and avoids stockouts. Too much inventory and too little inventory both create problems.

Track best sellers, slow movers, and reorder points. Count inventory regularly instead of trusting memory. Buy based on demand patterns, not only supplier pressure.
