## How can I improve accountability on my team?

Accountability improves when expectations, deadlines, and ownership are visible. It weakens when work is assigned vaguely.

Define who owns what and when it is due. Review results on a regular rhythm, not only when things fail. Correct missed commitments quickly and consistently.
