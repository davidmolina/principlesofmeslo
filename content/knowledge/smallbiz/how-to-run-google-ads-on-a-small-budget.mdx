## How can I run Google Ads with a small budget?

Small budgets work best when keywords are narrow and intent is high. Broad campaigns waste money quickly.

Target buyer-ready searches tied to one service or one location. Use negative keywords to block irrelevant clicks. Review search terms weekly and adjust based on results.
