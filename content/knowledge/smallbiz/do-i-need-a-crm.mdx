## Does a small business need a CRM?

Yes, once leads start slipping through the cracks or follow-up becomes inconsistent. A CRM helps you remember, track, and close.

Use it to track lead source, status, next step, and close rate. Keep the setup simple so the team actually uses it. Review the pipeline weekly to keep opportunities moving.
