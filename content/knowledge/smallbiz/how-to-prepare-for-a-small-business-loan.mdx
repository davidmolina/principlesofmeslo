## How do I prepare for a small-business loan application?

Lenders want proof that the business can repay the loan. Preparation is usually more important than the pitch itself.

Gather financial statements, tax returns, ownership documents, and use of funds. Show historical revenue or a realistic forecast tied to demand. Explain how the loan will improve sales, margin, or capacity.
