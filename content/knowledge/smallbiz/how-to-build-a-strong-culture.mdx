## How does a small business build a strong company culture?

Culture is how people behave when pressure shows up. It comes from repeated standards, not posters or slogans.

Define the behaviors you want to see and reinforce them consistently. Hire and promote people who match those standards. Make leadership behavior match what the company says it values.
