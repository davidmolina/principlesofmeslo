## How can I create invoices that get paid faster?

Invoices get paid faster when they are clear, prompt, and easy to act on. Confusing invoices create excuses and delays.

Send invoices immediately after the agreed milestone or delivery. Include scope reference, due date, payment options, and contact info. Use consistent numbering so questions can be resolved fast.
