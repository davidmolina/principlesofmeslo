## What is the best way to track business expenses?

Track expenses by category and attach proof while the details are still fresh. Good expense tracking supports taxes, pricing, and decision making.

Use one card or account for business spending whenever possible. Capture receipts and notes quickly with a repeatable process. Review categories monthly so waste and leaks become visible.
