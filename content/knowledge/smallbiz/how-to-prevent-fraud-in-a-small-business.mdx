## How can a small business prevent fraud?

Fraud drops when money movement has checks, visibility, and separation of duties. Trust is not a control system by itself.

Separate payment approval, bookkeeping, and bank reconciliation when possible. Review statements and unusual transactions regularly. Require documentation for refunds, purchases, and vendor changes.
