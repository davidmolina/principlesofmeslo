## What is the best way to handle customer complaints?

Handle complaints fast, calmly, and with clear next steps. A good recovery can preserve trust better than silence or defensiveness.

Listen fully before defending the business. Clarify the issue, timeline, and what resolution is realistic. Document the complaint so the team can prevent repeat issues.
