## Where should a small business post jobs?

Post where your ideal workers already look, and use your own network too. Better applicants often come from trusted referrals and local communities.

Use job boards, local colleges, trade groups, and community networks. Ask employees, customers, and partners for referrals. Write a clear posting so the right people self-select in.
