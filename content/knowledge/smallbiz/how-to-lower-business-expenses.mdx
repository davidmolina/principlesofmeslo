## How can I lower business expenses without hurting quality?

Start by cutting waste, not capability. Good cost reduction improves focus instead of weakening the business.

Audit recurring expenses and renegotiate where possible. Standardize purchasing so spending is not random. Protect the tools, labor, and marketing that actually drive results.
