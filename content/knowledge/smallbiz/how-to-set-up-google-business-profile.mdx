## How do I set up Google Business Profile for local marketing?

Google Business Profile is one of the fastest ways to appear in local searches. Complete profiles tend to earn more calls and direction requests.

Use your real business name, correct category, phone, and hours. Add photos, service areas, and a short keyword-relevant description. Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to them consistently.
