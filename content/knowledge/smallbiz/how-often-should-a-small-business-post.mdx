## How often should a small business post on social media?

Consistency matters more than volume. A simple posting schedule you can sustain beats bursts of activity followed by silence.

Choose a realistic cadence like two to four quality posts per week. Reuse common questions as recurring content themes. Track which posts create inquiries, not only likes.
