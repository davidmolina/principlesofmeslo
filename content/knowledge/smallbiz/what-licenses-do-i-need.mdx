## What business licenses or permits do I need?

Licenses depend on your city, state, industry, and whether you sell products or regulated services. Start local and confirm requirements before you open.

Check city, county, and state rules for your exact business type. Ask whether sales tax permits, health permits, or professional licenses apply. Track renewal dates so you do not fall out of compliance.
