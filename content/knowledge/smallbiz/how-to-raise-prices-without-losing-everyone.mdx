## How can I raise prices without losing all my customers?

Raise prices with clear reasoning, stronger positioning, and better service discipline. Price increases are easier when value is visible.

Increase prices on new customers first if needed. Explain changes around quality, cost, or service improvements. Do not apologize for pricing that keeps the business healthy.
