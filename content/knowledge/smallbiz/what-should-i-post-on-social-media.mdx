## What should a small business post on social media?

Post content that answers buyer questions, shows proof, and builds trust. Do not post only promotions.

Share before-and-after work, customer wins, tips, and behind-the-scenes moments. Use simple educational posts tied to the service you sell. End posts with one clear next step like call, message, or book.
