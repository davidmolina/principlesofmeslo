## How can I close more sales without discounting everything?

Higher close rates usually come from better qualification, clearer offers, and better follow-up. Discounting is often a shortcut for weak sales process.

Focus on buyers with real need, budget, and timing. Make your proposal easier to understand and easier to approve. Follow up with discipline until the deal is won or lost.
