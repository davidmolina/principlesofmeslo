## How should a small business analyze competitors?

Competitor analysis should reveal gaps, not trigger panic. The goal is to understand positioning, pricing, and where you can be more useful.

Compare offer, pricing, speed, reviews, and customer experience. Notice what buyers praise and what they complain about. Compete on clarity, trust, and execution instead of copying everything.
