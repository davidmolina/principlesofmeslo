## How can a small business control overhead better?

Control overhead by knowing which costs are essential, useful, or wasteful. Small leaks become major problems when revenue softens.

Review subscriptions, rent, admin costs, and owner habits monthly. Match overhead growth to real demand, not optimism. Remove costs that do not improve revenue, margin, or delivery.
