## What does a simple sales funnel look like for a small business?

A simple sales funnel moves people from awareness to inquiry to proposal to sale. It helps you find where leads stall.

Map the stages from first contact to paid customer. Measure how many people move from one stage to the next. Fix the biggest drop-off before adding more marketing.
