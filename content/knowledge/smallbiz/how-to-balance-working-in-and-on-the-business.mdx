## How do I balance working in the business and on the business?

Owners need time for delivery and time for strategy. If all your time goes into daily work, the business stays dependent on you.

Schedule weekly time for numbers, systems, and growth planning. Capture repetitive problems so you can solve them structurally. Delegate execution where possible so thinking time can exist.
