## How do I price a physical product?

Your product price needs to cover product cost, shipping, packaging, payment fees, marketing, and profit. Low pricing without margin discipline creates volume with no cash.

Know your landed cost before setting retail price. Test price points with real buyers, not only friends. Protect margin so discounts do not erase profit.
