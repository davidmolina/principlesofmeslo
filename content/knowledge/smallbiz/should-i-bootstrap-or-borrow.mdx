## Should I bootstrap my business or take a loan?

Bootstrap if you can prove demand with low cost and low risk. Borrow when capital clearly creates revenue and repayment is realistic.

Do not borrow to cover confusion or weak pricing. Use debt for equipment, inventory, or growth with visible return. Stress test repayment with conservative sales assumptions.
