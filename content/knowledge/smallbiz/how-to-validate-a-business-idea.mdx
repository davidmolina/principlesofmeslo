## How can I validate a business idea before I invest too much money?

Validation means real buyers show interest before you build too much. The goal is proof of demand, not compliments.

Interview potential customers and listen for repeated pain points. Offer a simple version of the service or product first. Measure replies, calls, deposits, and purchases instead of opinions.
