## How should I price my service business?

Price from your real costs, required profit, and market position. Guessing low to win work often creates cash problems later.

Calculate labor, materials, software, travel, taxes, and overhead. Add a target profit margin instead of pricing from emotion. Review jobs monthly to see which services are actually profitable.
