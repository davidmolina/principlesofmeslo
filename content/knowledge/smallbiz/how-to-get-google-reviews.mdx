## How can a small business get more Google reviews?

The best review strategy is to ask at the right moment with a simple process. Happy customers often need a direct reminder.

Ask after a positive result, delivery, or completed service. Send a direct review link by text or email. Train the team to ask consistently instead of occasionally.
