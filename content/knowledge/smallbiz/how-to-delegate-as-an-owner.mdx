## How do I delegate better as a business owner?

Delegation works when the expected result is clear, not just the task. Owners often delegate too late and too vaguely.

Explain outcome, deadline, quality standard, and decision limits. Start with repeatable work before delegating complex judgment calls. Review work early so mistakes do not multiply.
