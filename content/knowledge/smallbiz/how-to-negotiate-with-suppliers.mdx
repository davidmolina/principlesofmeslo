## How can a small business negotiate better with suppliers?

Negotiation improves when you know your volume, timing, alternatives, and payment history. Preparation matters more than pressure.

Ask about price breaks, payment terms, and delivery flexibility. Compare offers from multiple suppliers before committing. Build good relationships by paying on time and communicating clearly.
