## What key metrics should a small business track?

Track the few metrics that show demand, delivery, cash, and profit. Too many numbers can hide the important ones.

Watch leads, conversion rate, average sale, gross margin, and cash balance. Add overdue invoices, repeat customer rate, and response time if relevant. Review metrics on a weekly or monthly rhythm with action attached.
