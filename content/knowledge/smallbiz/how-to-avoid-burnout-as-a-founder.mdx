## How can a business owner avoid burnout?

Burnout often comes from unclear priorities, weak systems, and carrying too much alone for too long. Rest matters, but structure matters too.

Reduce repeated chaos by documenting and delegating recurring work. Watch workload, sleep, cash stress, and boundary problems honestly. Build support through peers, advisors, or trusted operators.
