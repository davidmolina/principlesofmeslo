## How can a small business protect customer data better?

Protecting data starts with simple controls and good habits. Small businesses get exposed more by weak basics than advanced attacks.

Use strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, and limited access. Store only the data you truly need. Train staff on phishing, scams, and safe handling of customer information.
