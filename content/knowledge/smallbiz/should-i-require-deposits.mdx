## Should I require a deposit before starting work?

Deposits reduce risk, improve cash flow, and confirm buyer commitment. Many small businesses wait too long to use them.

Use deposits for custom work, scheduling blocks, or large material exposure. State deposit terms clearly in proposals and invoices. Make deposit collection part of the sales process, not an afterthought.
