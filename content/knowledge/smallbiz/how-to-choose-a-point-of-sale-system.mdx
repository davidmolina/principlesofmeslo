## What should I look for in a point-of-sale system?

Your POS should fit how you sell, collect payment, and track inventory. A cheap system that creates friction is expensive in practice.

Check payment speed, reporting, inventory tools, and hardware needs. Make sure staff can learn it quickly. Confirm how it connects to accounting and ecommerce systems.
