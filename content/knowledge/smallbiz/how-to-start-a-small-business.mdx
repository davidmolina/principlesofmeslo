## How do I start a small business?

Start with one clear offer, one clear customer, and one simple way to get paid. Do not build a complicated company before you prove demand.

Define the problem you solve and who will pay for it. Choose a legal structure, open a business bank account, and separate finances. Set a simple price, start selling, and track every lead and sale.
