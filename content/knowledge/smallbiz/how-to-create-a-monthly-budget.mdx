## How should a small business build a monthly budget?

A monthly budget should tell you what you expect to earn, spend, and keep. It becomes useful when you compare plan versus actual every month.

List fixed costs, variable costs, debt payments, and owner pay. Set realistic sales targets based on current pipeline and seasonality. Review misses early so you can adjust before cash gets tight.
