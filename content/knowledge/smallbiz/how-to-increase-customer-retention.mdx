## How can I keep customers coming back?

Retention grows when customers get consistent value, clear communication, and a reason to return. Repeat customers are usually cheaper than new ones.

Follow up after delivery and look for ways to stay useful. Create recurring offers, reminders, or maintenance services. Study why customers leave and fix that before buying more ads.
