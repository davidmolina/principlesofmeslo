## How much money do I need to start a business?

You need enough to launch, sell, and survive the first slow period. Many founders underestimate working capital more than startup tools.

Separate one-time startup costs from monthly operating costs. Estimate at least a few months of cash needs before stable sales. Start lean and delay nonessential spending until demand is proven.
