## How can I manage my time better as a business owner?

Owner time improves when priorities are few and repeatable work is systemized. Constant context switching destroys momentum.

Block time for sales, operations, and review instead of reacting all day. Write down recurring tasks and delegate what others can do well. Protect high-value work from unnecessary interruptions.
