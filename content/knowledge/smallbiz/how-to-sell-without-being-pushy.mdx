## How can I sell my service without sounding pushy?

Good sales sounds like clear diagnosis, not pressure. Buyers respond better when you help them understand their problem and next step.

Ask questions before presenting a solution. Explain the cost of delay and the value of fixing the problem. Close with a simple option instead of a long speech.
