## How can I prepare for business taxes all year?

Tax prep works best as a weekly bookkeeping habit, not an annual scramble. Clean records reduce stress and mistakes.

Track income and expenses accurately every week. Save money for taxes as revenue comes in. Meet with a CPA before year end to avoid preventable surprises.
