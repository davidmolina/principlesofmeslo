## How can a small business build business credit?

Business credit builds through structure, reporting accounts, and consistent on-time payments. It takes repetition, not one big move.

Form the business, get an EIN, and use the business name consistently. Open vendor or credit accounts that report to business bureaus. Pay on time and keep balances controlled.
