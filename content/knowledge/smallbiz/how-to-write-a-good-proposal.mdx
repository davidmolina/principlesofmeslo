## What makes a good proposal for a small business?

A strong proposal removes confusion. It should explain scope, price, timeline, assumptions, and next steps in plain language.

Write the problem, solution, deliverables, and payment terms clearly. Avoid vague promises that create disputes later. End with a direct approval step so momentum is not lost.
