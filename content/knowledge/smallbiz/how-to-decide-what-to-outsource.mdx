## What should a small business outsource first?

Outsource specialized work, low-leverage admin, or tasks you consistently mishandle. Keep core customer value close.

Consider bookkeeping, payroll, design, or technical support first. Compare cost, speed, and quality before handing work off. Document expectations so outsourced work stays aligned.
