## Small Business Startup

The fastest way to start a business is to get a paying client. Then ensure that payment covers your costs and your profit margins are strong.

Remember the saying from Profit First author Mike Michalowicz: "revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, and cash is king." Read _Profit First_ if you want to structure your business with profit first in mind.

### Prompt

How do you start a business? What's the fastest way to start a business?
