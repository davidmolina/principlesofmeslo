## Federal Acquisition Regulation

The Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) is the primary set of rules governing how the federal government purchases goods and services.

### Why It Matters

All federal contractors must follow FAR requirements when submitting bids and performing contracts.

### Prompt

How does the FAR influence the way contractors prepare proposals and execute projects?
