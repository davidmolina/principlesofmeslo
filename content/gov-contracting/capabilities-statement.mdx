## Capabilities Statement

A capabilities statement is a one-page document summarizing a company’s services, experience, and qualifications.

### Why It Matters

Government agencies and prime contractors often request capabilities statements during vendor outreach.

### Prompt

How can I create a strong capabilities statement for my business?

### Key Elements

- Core competencies
- Past performance
- Differentiators
- Contact information
