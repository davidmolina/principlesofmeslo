## RFI – Scope Clarification

RFIs are commonly used when project documents contain ambiguous or conflicting scope descriptions.

### Template

Subject: RFI – Scope Clarification – [Project Name]

Dear [Contracting Officer],

While reviewing the contract documents for **[Project Name]**, we identified a potential scope clarification.

**Reference:**  
Specification Section: [example: 23 05 00]

**Observation:**  
The specification references [describe the issue].

**Request:**  
Please confirm whether the scope should include [describe potential interpretation].

Clarifying this item will help ensure accurate pricing and project coordination.

Sincerely,

[Name]  
[Company]  
[Contact Information]
