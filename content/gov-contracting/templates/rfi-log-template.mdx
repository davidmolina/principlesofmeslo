## RFI Log Template

Track every RFI in one place to reduce missed clarifications and unresolved scope risk.

### Suggested Columns

- RFI Number
- Date Submitted
- Submitted By
- Subject
- Drawing/Spec Reference
- Submitted To (COR/CO/Design Team)
- Response Due Date
- Response Received Date
- Status (`Open`, `Answered`, `Closed`)
- Schedule Impact
- Cost Impact
- Change Order Required (`Yes/No`)
- Notes
