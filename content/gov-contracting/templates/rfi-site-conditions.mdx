## RFI – Site Conditions

Contractors often submit RFIs when site conditions appear different from the drawings.

### Template

Subject: RFI – Site Conditions – [Project Name]

Dear [Contracting Officer],

During our site review for **[Project Name]**, we observed conditions that may differ from the construction documents.

**Observation:**  
[Describe site condition]

**Reference:**  
Drawing or Spec: [example: C-102]

**Request:**  
Please confirm whether the existing condition should be addressed as part of the project scope.

Your clarification will help ensure accurate pricing and planning.

Respectfully,

[Name]  
[Company]
