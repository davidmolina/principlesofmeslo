## RFI – Construction Phase

RFIs are frequently submitted during project execution when additional clarification is needed.

### Template

Subject: RFI – Construction Clarification – [Project Name]

Dear [Contracting Officer Representative],

We are requesting clarification regarding the following item encountered during construction.

**Reference:**  
Drawing / Specification: [reference number]

**Issue:**  
[Describe the issue encountered]

**Proposed Resolution:**  
[Optional contractor recommendation]

Please advise on the appropriate direction so that work may proceed without delay.

Sincerely,

[Name]  
[Company]
