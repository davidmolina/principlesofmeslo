## RFI Response Review Template

Use this template to evaluate whether an RFI response is complete and actionable before field execution.

### Review Checklist

- Response clearly answers the specific question.
- Response references exact drawing/spec revision.
- Scope impact is identified (`none`, `minor`, `major`).
- Schedule impact is identified.
- Cost impact is identified.
- Change order requirement is identified.
- Field team and estimator are notified.
- Master Takeoff updates are logged (if applicable).
- Documentation is filed in project records.
