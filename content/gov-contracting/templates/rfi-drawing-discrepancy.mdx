## RFI – Drawing Discrepancy

Sometimes drawings conflict with written specifications.

### Template

Subject: RFI – Drawing Discrepancy – [Project Name]

Dear [Project Manager],

Our team identified a discrepancy between the drawings and specifications.

**Reference:**  
Drawing: [example: A-201]  
Specification Section: [example: 09 21 16]

**Issue:**  
The drawing indicates [describe condition], while the specification indicates [describe alternate condition].

**Request:**  
Please confirm which requirement should govern for bidding purposes.

Thank you for your guidance.

Best regards,

[Name]  
[Company]  
[Contact Information]
