## Formal RFI Letter

Some agencies require RFIs to be submitted in formal letter format.

### Letter Template

Date: [Insert Date]

Project: [Project Name]  
Solicitation Number: [Number]

To: [Contracting Officer]

Subject: Request for Information

Dear [Name],

Our firm is preparing a proposal for the above referenced project and respectfully requests clarification regarding the following item.

Reference: [Drawing / Specification]

Question:  
[Describe the issue or question]

We appreciate your assistance and look forward to your response.

Respectfully,

[Name]  
[Company Name]
