## Request for Information (RFI) – Basic Email Template

Contractors often submit RFIs to clarify drawings, specifications, or scope requirements during the bidding phase.

### Email Template

Subject: RFI – [Project Name] – Clarification on [Specification or Drawing]

Dear [Contracting Officer / Project Manager],

Our team is currently reviewing the solicitation documents for **[Project Name / Solicitation Number]**.

We would appreciate clarification regarding the following item:

**Reference:**  
Drawing / Specification: [example: Sheet C-3, Detail 2]

**Question:**  
Please confirm whether [describe the clarification needed].

This information will help ensure our proposal aligns with the project requirements.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Respectfully,

[Name]  
[Title]  
[Company Name]  
[Phone]  
[Email]
