## Pricing Strategy

Government pricing must balance competitiveness with realistic cost estimates.

### Why It Matters

Underpricing can lead to financial losses, while overpricing reduces competitiveness.

### Prompt

How should my business structure pricing when pursuing government contracts?

### Guidance

Develop a clear cost structure including labor, materials, overhead, and profit. Use the MESLO framework to ensure your direct, indirect and G&A costs are included.
