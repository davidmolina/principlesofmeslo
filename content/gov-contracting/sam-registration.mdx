## SAM Registration

The System for Award Management (SAM.gov) is the official database used by the federal government to track vendors.

### Why It Matters

Without an active SAM registration, a company cannot receive federal contract awards.

### Prompt

What steps should I take to register my company in SAM.gov?

### Key Steps

- Create a SAM.gov account.
- Obtain a Unique Entity Identifier (UEI).
- Complete business registration details.
- Schedule an appointment with an APEX procurement advisor.
