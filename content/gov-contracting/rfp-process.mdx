## Request for Proposal (RFP)

An RFP is a document issued by an agency requesting proposals for a specific project.

### Why It Matters

Submitting strong RFP responses is essential for winning contracts.

### Prompt

How can I structure a competitive response to an RFP?

### Key Components

- Technical approach
- Past performance
- Pricing strategy
