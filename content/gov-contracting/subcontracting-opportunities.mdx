## Working with Prime Contractors

Small businesses often enter government contracting by partnering with larger prime contractors.

### Why It Matters

Subcontracting allows smaller firms to gain experience and past performance.

### Prompt

How can my company build relationships with prime contractors?

### Strategies

- Attend industry days
- Connect with procurement officers
- Register in subcontracting databases
