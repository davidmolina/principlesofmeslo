## Where to Find Contracts

Federal contract opportunities are posted on official government procurement portals.

### Why It Matters

Monitoring these portals helps businesses identify upcoming solicitations.

### Prompt

Where should I search for federal contracting opportunities?

### Key Sources

- SAM.gov opportunities
- Agency procurement portals
- Prime contractor subcontracting portals

Then add these to your bid log, have a bid meeting, and bid until you win.
