## Small Business Set-Asides

The U.S. government reserves certain contracts specifically for small businesses.

### Why It Matters

Set-aside programs help smaller firms compete against larger companies.

### Prompt

Which small business certifications could help my company qualify for set-aside opportunities?

### Examples

- Small business
- 8(a) program
- HUBZone
- Service-disabled veteran-owned
- Woman-owned small business
