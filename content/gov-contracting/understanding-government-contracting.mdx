## What is Government Contracting?

Government contracting refers to the process of private companies providing goods or services to federal, state, or local government agencies.

For small businesses, it represents one of the largest and most stable markets in the world.

### Why It Matters

The U.S. federal government alone spends hundreds of billions of dollars each year on contracts.

### Prompt

How can my small business begin exploring government contracting opportunities?

### Key Steps

- Identify your service or product category.
- Register your business with federal systems (`sam.gov`).
- Research agencies that buy what you sell (`APEX`).
