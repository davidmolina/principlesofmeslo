## Retainage

Retainage is a portion of payment withheld until a project or milestone is completed.

### Why It Matters

Retainage encourages contractors and subcontractors to complete the work properly and resolve any remaining issues.

### Risk Mitigation

Clear retainage terms in contracts help avoid disputes and ensure all work is finished before final payment.
