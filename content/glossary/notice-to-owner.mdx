## Notice to Owner

A Notice to Owner informs the property owner that a subcontractor or supplier is providing labor or materials to a project.

### Why It Matters

This notice preserves the right to file a lien if payment issues arise.

### Risk Mitigation

Providing proper notice early in a project protects the legal rights of subcontractors and suppliers.
