## Performance Bond

A performance bond guarantees that a contractor will complete the project according to the contract terms.

### Why It Matters

If the contractor fails to perform, the bond provider can step in to complete the work or compensate the project owner.

### Risk Mitigation

Performance bonds reduce financial risk for project owners and ensure projects are completed.
