## Material Lien

A material lien, often called a **mechanic's lien**, is a legal claim placed on a property by a contractor, subcontractor, or supplier who has not been paid for work or materials provided to a project.

Material liens exist to protect those who contribute labor or materials by giving them the right to seek payment through the property itself.

### Why It Matters

Material liens help ensure that contractors, subcontractors, and suppliers are paid for the work they perform. If payment disputes arise, the lien creates a legal path to recover funds.

### Risk Mitigation

Contractors and project owners reduce lien risk by:

- using clear contracts
- tracking payments carefully
- collecting lien waivers from subcontractors and suppliers
- maintaining accurate project documentation

### Protecting Your Project

Understanding how material liens work helps protect both contractors and owners. When payment flows and documentation are well managed, projects move forward without costly legal disputes.
