## Lien Waiver

A lien waiver is a document that confirms a contractor, subcontractor, or supplier has received payment and agrees to waive the right to file a lien for that amount.

### Why It Matters

Lien waivers help prevent future payment disputes and protect the property owner from unexpected claims.

### Risk Mitigation

Contractors typically collect lien waivers with each progress payment to ensure all parties have been paid properly.
