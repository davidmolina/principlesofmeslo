## Bid Bond

A bid bond guarantees that a contractor who wins a bid will enter into the contract and provide required performance and payment bonds.

### Why It Matters

Bid bonds protect project owners from contractors who submit bids but fail to proceed with the project.

### Risk Mitigation

They ensure serious and financially capable bidders participate in the process.
