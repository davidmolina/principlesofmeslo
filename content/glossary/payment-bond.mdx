## Payment Bond

A payment bond guarantees that subcontractors, laborers, and suppliers will be paid for their work.

### Why It Matters

Payment bonds protect workers and suppliers if the contractor fails to make payments.

### Risk Mitigation

On public projects, payment bonds are often required to protect all parties involved in the construction process.
