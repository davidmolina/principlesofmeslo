## Office Overhead

Office overhead includes rent, utilities, and administrative salaries. The value of time (t) is included in overhead and should account for the owner's time as well. Thus overhead is both owner's time and G&A expenses to run the business. Use the overhead section in your master takeoff to determine your OH/daily and OH/project cost.
