## Material Takeoff

A material takeoff measures the quantities of materials required for a project. Anything being bolted, installed, sprayed, applied or staying onsite goes in your materials section in the master takeoff.

### Why It Matters

Accurate quantities form the foundation of reliable estimates.

### Prompt

How should estimators organize material takeoffs for complex construction projects?
