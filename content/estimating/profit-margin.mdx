## Profit Margin

Profit margin represents revenue remaining after all project costs are paid. This differs from profit markup. Markup is the percentage added on top of cost to determine the selling price while margin is profit expressed as a percentage of the final selling price.

### Conversion Formulas

**Markup → Margin**

`margin = markup / (1 + markup)`

**Margin → Markup**

`markup = margin / (1 - margin)`

### Example Conversion

Desired margin = `20%` (`0.20`)

Required markup:

`0.20 / (1 - 0.20) = 0.25`  → markup required = `25%`
