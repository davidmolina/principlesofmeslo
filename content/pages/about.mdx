# About the Principles of MESLO

The Principles of MESLO is a practical system for construction estimating and operational planning.

MESLO stands for Material, Equipment, Subcontractors, Labor, and Other Costs, with estimating discipline that also accounts for Insurance/Bond, Overhead, Profit, and Taxes.

The goal is to help builders reduce guesswork, improve pricing clarity, and align estimating decisions with real-world execution.

This platform supports the book with framework guidance, glossary definitions, templates, and assistant-ready knowledge so teams can apply the concepts in day-to-day operations.
