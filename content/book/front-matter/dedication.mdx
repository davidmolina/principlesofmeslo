This book is dedicated to my old sergeant, George J. Everts III (1966-2022), a career U.S. Army soldier, infantryman, drill sergeant, recruiter, and trusted mentor. We first served together more than twenty years ago when I was a young Private First Class in the 104th Division in Salem, Oregon, where he helped shape my early development as a soldier and leader.

Over the years, Everts served around the world, including Operation Desert Storm, where he earned the Combat Infantryman Badge, and later continued his service through volunteer work with Operation Code, helping expand opportunities for veterans entering the technology workforce. He eventually joined me at Molinas Construction Company, where he became our first in-house principal estimator, bringing his Army leadership, field experience, and practical judgment to our work.

George demonstrated that disciplined estimating does not require credentials alone, it requires clarity, common sense, and a deep understanding of the operating environment. He is deeply missed by those who had the privilege of knowing and serving with him.
