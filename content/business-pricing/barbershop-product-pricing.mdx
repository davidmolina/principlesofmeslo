## Barbershop Product Pricing

Barbershop product pricing should reflect brand position, reorder frequency, and attach rate per haircut.

### Why It Matters

Products can increase average ticket when pricing is simple and recommendation-driven.

### Prompt

How can barbershops price pomades, sprays, and grooming kits effectively?
