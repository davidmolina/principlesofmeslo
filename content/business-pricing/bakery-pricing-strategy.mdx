## Bakery Pricing Strategy

Bakery pricing should account for ingredient cost, labor, packaging, spoilage, and daily demand cycles.

### Why It Matters

Low-margin bakery products can become unprofitable quickly when waste and labor are ignored.

### Prompt

How should a bakery structure pricing for breads, pastries, and custom orders?
