## Ecommerce Product Pricing

Ecommerce pricing should include landed cost, fulfillment fees, return rate, and advertising spend.

### Why It Matters

Many online sellers underprice by excluding CAC and returns from unit economics.

### Prompt

How should ecommerce brands calculate true profitable selling price?
