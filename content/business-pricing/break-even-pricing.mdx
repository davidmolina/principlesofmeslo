## Break-Even Pricing

Break-even pricing identifies the minimum price needed to cover fixed and variable costs at a target sales volume.

### Why It Matters

It prevents underpricing and improves planning for promotions, launches, and growth.

### Prompt

How can businesses calculate break-even price under different volume scenarios?
