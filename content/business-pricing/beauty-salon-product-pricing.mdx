## Beauty Salon Product Pricing

Salon retail pricing should account for wholesale cost, shrinkage, and expected sell-through time.

### Why It Matters

Retail products can improve revenue per client only if priced and stocked deliberately.

### Prompt

How should salons price in-store products while preserving client trust?
