## Pizzeria Pricing Strategy

Pizzeria pricing should reflect dough yield, topping cost, labor minutes, and delivery commission impact.

### Why It Matters

Third-party delivery fees can significantly reduce net margin per order.

### Prompt

How should pizzerias structure price tiers by size and topping complexity?
