## Wholesale Pricing for Small Brands

Wholesale pricing requires margin room for both brand and retailer while preserving market competitiveness.

### Why It Matters

If wholesale is set too low, growth can increase sales while reducing profitability.

### Prompt

How should small brands set wholesale and retail price ladders?
