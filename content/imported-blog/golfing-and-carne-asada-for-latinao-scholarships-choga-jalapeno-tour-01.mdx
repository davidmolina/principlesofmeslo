Last weekend, I had the pleasure of competing in the CHOGA's (Chicanos of Oregon Golf Association), 33rd Annual Jalapeno Tour at Sunset Grove, near Forest Grove. The nine-hole course was a lot of fun and I met a lot of great people from all over Oregon and Washington State, all there for a great cause. The 2-day tour is an annual fundraiser for Latina/o scholarships, and organized by veteran Chicano activists that were involved with the Colegio Cesar Chavez back in the 70s. While I had known the group existed, Dan Lucero, brought it back up to our attention recently and got us connected with co-organizer Jose Romero.
