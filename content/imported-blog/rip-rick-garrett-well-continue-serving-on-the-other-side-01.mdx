This past Thursday, a former co-worker and friend from my previous active-duty military assignment, The Joint Personal Effects Depot (JPED) served God on this earth one last time and it pains me he's gone. The JPED is ground zero for processing the personal belongings & cherished memories for nearly every American killed-in-action or wounded from the Wars in Iraq & Afghanistan. When JPED was stood up after the 9/11 Pentagon attacks at Fort Myers, Virginia and later relocated to Aberdeen Proving Ground (APG), Maryland, Rick Garrett was one of the first non-uniforms on the ground to help document this part of 21st century military history. A trained & professional photographer, he captured the memories as the personal belongings arrived off the trucks, unpacked, properly prepared and moved along for final outbound shipment.
