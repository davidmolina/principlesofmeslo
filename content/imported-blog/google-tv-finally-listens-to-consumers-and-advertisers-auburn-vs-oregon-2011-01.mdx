As we sit here as a family watching the [Oregon Auburn 2011 game](http://twitter.com/search/Oregon%20Auburn%20game#) on the big screen courtesy of GoogleTV I'm in awe. The picture is crisp. The streaming is flying. And, most importantly the content is not blocked. Unlike streaming the internet on our computers, GoogleTV prevents us a similar positive and seamless experience. Since we purchased it, we've watched mostly Netflix and a number of Blu-Ray's--most of the content we're used to is blocked, including MSNBC and most major sport events. An almost total waste. With three very young children we knew watching this historic game would be out of the question outside the house or in another home.
