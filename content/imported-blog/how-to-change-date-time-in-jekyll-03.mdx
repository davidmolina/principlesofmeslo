while all my commits were back, the date for my grandfather's obituary was still indicating May 2nd. To fix this, change UTC in our Jekyll blog post from: ```javascript date: 2023-05-03 17:12:43 -0800 ``` to: ```javascript date: 2023-05-03 11:12:43 -0800 ``` The UTC time for the first one puts the blog post to the next day.
