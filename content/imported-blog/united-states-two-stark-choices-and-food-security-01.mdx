The United States faces two stark choices: grow and control U.S. food security within our borders or import from abroad often with zero or little environmental oversight. In 2022 the United States fell backwards on food exports for the first time -- now importing more food for the American consumer than exporting food. America's family farms, small and large, provide for here and abroad, but America’s agriculture-based economy is being threatened. The Russia-Ukraine war has drawn a harsh reminder that in a globalized food economy we're vulnerable. Compromising America's food supply has irreversible, long-term U.S. national and regional security implications when we can't guarantee stable food supply and agriculture jobs.
