As the Founder & Executive Director your job is three fold: communicate the vision and mission to everyone you come across, recruit and retain volunteers (this includes new board members), and ensure there's enough money in the bank at all times to execute on the mission. In summary, move expeditiously. Hope this list helps anyone considering launching a nonprofit startup. And, good luck Alexander on launching yours!
