Now I'm not the first to pen open criticism of our nation's Veterans Affairs (VA) Medical Centers, whether its Walter Reed or Portland VA Medical Center. Today, for the first time [I went in to get seen at the emergency room](https://foursquare.com/v/portland-va-medical-center/4bbf3fd9ba9776b07cc2fec8) on a lower back pain that came back this week with a vengeance--my hope was to receive an steroid injection (SI) as the military doctors had done previously at Bethesda/Walter Reed--but after being seen I was prescribed meds, and a follow up in two weeks. Up to this point I was pleased and impressed with the genuine patient care and compassion exhibited from the front desk, volunteers, nurses to the doctors. My next stop. Pharmacy.
