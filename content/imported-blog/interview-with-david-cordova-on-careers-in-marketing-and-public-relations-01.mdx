In this podcast, David Córdova of David Córdova & Associates, LLC, a marketing and public relations, shares his thoughts and insights on struggles and barriers Latinos face everyday. A former journalist and Telemundo executive, Córdova shares his passion for ensuring Latino and all student success and the powerful experience that comes with attending the United States Hispanic Leadership Institute (USHLI), Annual National Convention. Hope you will be inspired as we were. Enjoy! [Originally posted on Posterous](http://molina.posterous.com/)
