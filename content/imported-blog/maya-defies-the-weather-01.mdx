Over the past week our daughter, Maya, has been feeling under the weather. It's been difficult as parents trying to get some shuteye, but more so, it's been even more difficult for her. She knows and hates a runny nose. Calls it "mocos" and demands that her mother or I wipe them off. Her pediatrician has also been concerned because she's not gaining the weight that she is supposed to as a toddler. They've asked us to feed her more foods with lots of fatty foods, i.e. cottage cheese, cheese, peanut butter, etc. I'll admit Maya has her preferences and she'll eat plenty when she loves the foods we give her, but how do you guarantee or how do we know as parents that she is where she is supposed to be in weight?
