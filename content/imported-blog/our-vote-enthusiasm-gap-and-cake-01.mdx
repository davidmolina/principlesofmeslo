Monday morning, I skipped gym at 24 Hour Fitness to drop our ballots off in Portland. We had been sitting on them over the weekend and hadn't even completed them (Edith was early on this one) until last, Friday. It wasn't a challenge at all. Having three very small children in the house is. Over the past few week our home, like your home I imagine, has been swamped by political mail-- vote for me--he's evil--type literature. Political messages. Endorsements. Marketing collateral. Facebook ads, TV and radio ads. Honestly, you either like the guy/gal or you don't. You either trust him/her or you don't. You either supported the measure based on what you believe to be true, what you read in the voter's pamphlet or how your friends/family voted.
