Many combat patches abound, the most common the Multinational Security Transitions Command, Iraq (MNSTC-I), the patch worn by Veterans' of Task Force Desert Wolf of 104th Division IT and other Soldiers from across the United States that deployed to Iraq between 2007-2008. Driving north on I-5 this morning I listened intently to NPR Weekend and the coverage of 9/11 remembrances, from New York to the Pentagon. That solemn date which has been cemented into our collective memories, much like the names of many who went through JPED cemented into those who served and continue to serve there.
