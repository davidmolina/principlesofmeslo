Last week I sat down with my State Senator. Senator Laurie Monnes-Anderson and I met for about an hour at the Gresham City Hall, tucked between two reception areas in the Multnomah Room, we discussed issues concerning area Hispanics and my take (the majority are no different than non-Hispanic issues). I requested the meeting and was pleased she followed through. The meeting was prompted after being re-confirmed by Governor Ted Kulongoski to the Oregon Commission on Hispanic Affairs (www.oregon.gov/hispanic) for another 3-year term. I requested a meeting with my two representatives. I've not met with Rep. Nick Kahl, yet. To summarize the conversation we discussed: 1. Drivers' License, 2. Gang Violence, 3. K-12 Education and College, 4. Health Care and 5. Jobs. On the drivers' license I expressed my concern that the current law undermines public safety for all Oregonians.
