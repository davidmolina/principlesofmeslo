We've been on the road since Saturday evening when my father-in-law, former Pastor Juan Quiroz packed our bags, kissed/hugged our wife's & kids goodbye, made one last prayer and departed The Dalles, Oregon. After trekking through eastern Oregon and Idaho we finally stopped for some shuteye in Utah. It was early in the morning so we passed on the fancy hotel and rested for a few hours next to some CATs and gas station. The snow capped mountains that lined the interstate were beautiful and the air was crisp. The next morning we hauled through mountainous Wyoming. The roads seemed to turn to fog at times due to the fast blowing snow along the edge of the road.
