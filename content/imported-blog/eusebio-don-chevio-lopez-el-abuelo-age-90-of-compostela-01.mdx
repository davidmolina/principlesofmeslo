Eusebio "Don Chevio" Lopez, age 90 of Compostela, Nayarit passed away peacefully at his home on Monday, May 1st, 2023 surrounded by family. He was born September 11th, 1932 to Aurelio Flores and Catalina Lopez in Embocadero, Nayarit. He grew up working as a farmhand and later in life as bartender and small business owner. Eusebio loved gardening, fishing, traveling, and making the annual trek to Talpa de Allende, Jalisco.
