A trained & professional photographer, he captured the memories as the personal belongings arrived off the trucks, unpacked, properly prepared and moved along for final outbound shipment. He would later train other non-uniform and uniform service-members on photographing the inventory process. A perfectionist who shared his love of the camera--and equally his love of golf, but mostly how he wanted to serve our nation & her patriots in her greatest time of need. I once asked Rick, "you could be doing anything else, but you're here right in the thick of it." His response: "I love it here. This is one way I can serve those who have served us."
