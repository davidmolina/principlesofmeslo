Yesterday evening, I had the pleasure of speaking before Latina/o parents and their students at Woodburn High School. The evening was packed with OSU departments, from admissions to financial aid, from honors college to the college of health and human sciences. An entire delegation of faculty, staff and students came out, and it was nice to see OSU Chicana/o and Latina/o students leaders from their respective organizations engage with high school students. It was as if OSU picked up and moved its entire operation to Woodburn. It felt nice. I met several parents, one in particular, Jose Rojo, inspired the possibilities. Rojo immigrated to the U.S. over 25 years ago and had originally studied engineering back home and built a successful business.
