1. Drivers' License, 2. Gang Violence, 3. K-12 Education and College, 4. Health Care and 5. Jobs. On the drivers' license I expressed my concern that the current law undermines public safety for all Oregonians. Currently, Oregon requires proof of citizenship to take the exam and qualify for a drivers' license. This law hugely impacted a part of Oregon's Latino community. We also discussed Hispanic gang violence and outreach. I mentioned a few years ago, former Multnomah County Commissioner Serena Cruz led a Latina/o Youth Gang Summit in Gresham. What we found was that Latina/o youth were often targeted by law enforcement if they either a. dressed a certain way, and/or b. associated with known gang members.
