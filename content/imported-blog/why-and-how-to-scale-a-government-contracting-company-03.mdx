Margins. Prior to launching your business, and definitely prior to the end of your project, the company should evaluate the profit & loss by jobs to analyze which divisions, which projects are the most profitable and which are the most stressful. I shared with the group how in the past I was not only losing my hair but my vitals were through the roof. It's both unhealthy, unwise and irresponsible to continue to trek down this path when the numbers (margins) can be better elsewhere and you get to keep your hair. Pivot. Simplify your business. Use an accounting software, a great bookkeeper and CPA to advise accordingly. Network. Your network is your net worth.
