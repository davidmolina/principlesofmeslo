I would never have thought that I would run a nonprofit, but I learned early on that entrepreneurship isn’t just profit and grow, grow, grow… I think often times we look at entrepreneurship and think, ‘We need to build the next unicorn.’ No, you just need to build something that is of value to others. Whatever you build, build something that changes the lives of many people.”
