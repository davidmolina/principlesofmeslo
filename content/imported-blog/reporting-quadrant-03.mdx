### Reporting Quadrant: ```javascript - __________|__________ | - Top left: Working on now - Top right: Working on next few - Bottom left: Next few months - Bottom right: What is keeping me up at night ``` The briefing tool is designed to be interactive, including: - Few bullet items per quadrant, one slide total - Quantitive and qualitative data points, descriptions - What’s on the burner now and where is our attention going - Provides team members opportunity to engage, discover and troubleshoot - Everyone in the briefing room learns quickly - The senior or owner adds value on the last quadrant by un-bottlenecking issues which builds further momentum The key is to make updates informative, organized and provide an opportunity to discover bottlenecks. Entrepreneurs and business owners have the most influence here. This is where they should live
