This morning on our riverwalk and observing the sunset, reflecting and taking it in we stopped by a small coffee shop. The business, a moving trailer was retrofitted with a full barista bar and the opening was a tiny open source library. A few items to note: - Every conceivable coffee and specialty drink could be prepared - If you only focused on the interior and friendly customer service you wouldn't think you were ordering from a trailer - The open library provided the ability to read while your drink was prepared and $8.00 if you chose to buy it As I talked with the owners, I picked up several books to skim through, including Hoyle's Rules of Games by Albert Morehead & Geoffrey Mott-Smith, 1215: The Year of Magna Carta by Danny Danziger and John Gillingham, and Man's Search
