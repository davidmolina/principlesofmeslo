Over the weekend at my oldest quinceñera my brother Victor told us about something he'd discovered and it was working wonders on his sleep. It's a simple hack requiring little or no effort at all. The answer: turning your phone on airplane mode while you sleep and storing it at least an arms length away. Steps on iPhone: ```javascript - Settings > - Airplane Mode > turn on - Place arms length away and go to sleep ``` Our bodies and brains are electrical and that by shutting off all electromagnetic waves, our brains are able to rest.
