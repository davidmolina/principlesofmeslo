## Tabor 100

Tabor 100 is a nonprofit organization supporting Black-owned businesses and entrepreneurs in Washington State.

### What They Do

The organization focuses on:

- economic empowerment
- networking opportunities
- mentorship
- access to corporate and government procurement

### Why It Matters

Community organizations like Tabor 100 help small businesses build relationships that lead to contract opportunities.
