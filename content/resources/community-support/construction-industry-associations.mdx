## Construction Industry Associations

Industry associations provide networking, advocacy, and training for contractors.

### Examples

- Associated General Contractors (AGC)
- National Association of Minority Contractors (NAMC)
- local contractor associations

### Why It Matters

Associations connect contractors with project opportunities and industry knowledge.

### Prompt

How can contractors leverage industry associations for business development?
