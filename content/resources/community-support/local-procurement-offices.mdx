## Local Procurement Offices

Many cities, counties, and states maintain procurement offices that help businesses understand how to compete for public contracts.

### What They Provide

- vendor registration assistance
- upcoming contract opportunities
- training workshops

### Why It Matters

Local government contracts can be an entry point for contractors new to public procurement.

### Prompt

How can contractors register with local procurement systems?
