## National Association of Minority Contractors (NAMC)

The National Association of Minority Contractors supports minority-owned construction firms through advocacy, training, and business development.

### What They Do

NAMC chapters help contractors by providing:

- networking with general contractors and developers
- training and education programs
- access to procurement opportunities
- industry advocacy

### Why It Matters

Minority-owned construction companies often face barriers in accessing capital and major projects. NAMC helps bridge those gaps.
