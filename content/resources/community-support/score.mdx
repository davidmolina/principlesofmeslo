## SCORE Mentoring

SCORE is a network of volunteer business mentors that supports small business owners through mentoring and education.

### Why It Matters for Contractors

The right mentor can help you tighten:

- pricing logic and financial targets
- operational roles and reporting
- marketing and pipeline discipline
- decision-making under uncertainty

### Link

Find a mentor: https://www.score.org/find-mentor
