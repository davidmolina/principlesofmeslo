## Women’s Business Centers (WBCs)

Women’s Business Centers (WBCs) provide business training and advising to support women entrepreneurs.

### Why It Matters for Contractors

WBCs can help with:

- business planning and structure
- operational systems and documentation
- financing readiness and growth planning
