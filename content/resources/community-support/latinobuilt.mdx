## LatinoBuilt

LatinoBuilt describes itself as Oregon and Southwest Washington's largest trade association focused on advancing Latino-owned construction businesses and Latinos in construction.

### What They Do

- bilingual workshops and trainings
- business advisory and technical assistance
- networking, mentoring, and hosted events
- advocacy and access to professional resources

### Why It Matters

LatinoBuilt is built around contractor growth, professional pathways, and long-term business ownership for Latino contractors and related firms in the construction industry.
