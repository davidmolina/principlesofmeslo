## MESO - Micro Enterprise Services of Oregon

MESO provides business training, financial coaching, and capital access to small businesses across Oregon.

### What They Do

Programs include:

- business planning assistance
- financial literacy training
- small business loans
- entrepreneur mentorship

### Why It Matters

MESO supports early-stage entrepreneurs and contractors who need help building sustainable businesses.
