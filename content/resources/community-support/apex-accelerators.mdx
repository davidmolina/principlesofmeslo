## APEX Accelerators

APEX Accelerators (formerly Procurement Technical Assistance Centers, or PTACs) help small businesses pursue government contracting opportunities.

### What They Do

APEX Accelerators provide free or low-cost assistance with:

- SAM.gov registration
- capability statements
- identifying contract opportunities
- proposal preparation
- federal compliance guidance

### Why It Matters

Government contracting systems can be difficult for new businesses to navigate. APEX advisors help contractors understand procurement requirements and improve their chances of winning work.
