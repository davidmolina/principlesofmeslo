## Subcontracting Pathways (Prime Contractors)

Many small contractors build past performance by subcontracting under prime contractors.

### Why It Matters

Subcontracting can help you:

- gain experience with compliance and documentation expectations
- build a track record and references
- learn how primes structure scope and reporting

### Prompt

How can small contractors position themselves for subcontracting opportunities with primes?
