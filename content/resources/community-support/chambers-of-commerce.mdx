## Chambers of Commerce (Local Business Networks)

Local chambers of commerce are community business networks that help members build relationships, referrals, and visibility.

### Why It Matters for Contractors

Contractors win work through trust. Chambers can help you:

- build relationships with property managers, owners, and local businesses
- find local events and supplier connections
- get warm introductions for early projects

How can contractors use chambers of commerce to generate referrals and partnerships?
