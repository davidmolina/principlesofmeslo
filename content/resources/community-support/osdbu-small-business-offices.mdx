## OSDBU and Small Business Offices (Federal Agencies)

Many federal agencies maintain offices that support small business participation in contracting. These offices often host outreach events and provide guidance on how the agency buys.

### Why It Matters

Contractors can learn:

- what the agency buys most often
- typical contract structures and requirements
- where to find upcoming opportunities
