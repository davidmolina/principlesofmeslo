## SBA 8(a) Program

The 8(a) Business Development Program helps socially and economically disadvantaged businesses compete for federal contracts.

### Benefits

- access to set-aside contracts
- mentorship programs
- business development support

### Why It Matters

The program creates opportunities for small firms to work directly with federal agencies.

### Prompt

How does the SBA 8(a) program help contractors win federal work?
