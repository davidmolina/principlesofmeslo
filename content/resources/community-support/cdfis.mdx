## Community Development Financial Institutions

CDFIs are mission-driven lenders that provide capital to underserved businesses and communities.

### What They Provide

- small business loans
- startup capital
- financial coaching

### Why It Matters

Contractors often need access to working capital to pursue larger projects.

### Prompt

How can contractors use CDFI financing to support project growth?
