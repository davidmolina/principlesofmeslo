## Minority Business Development Agency

The MBDA supports minority-owned businesses through capital access, contract opportunities, and strategic partnerships.

### What They Provide

- business consulting
- capital connections
- procurement assistance

### Why It Matters

MBDA centers help minority-owned contractors expand into larger markets.

### Prompt

How can minority contractors work with MBDA centers to grow their businesses?
