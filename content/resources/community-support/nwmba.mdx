## Northwest Minority Builders Alliance (NWMBA)

NWMBA is a locally controlled Washington State nonprofit business league serving the Pacific Northwest and focused on minority business enterprises in construction and related industries.

### What They Do

- connections across government, corporate, and community sectors
- advocacy for fair and equitable public policy
- training and technical assistance
- support for certification, pre-award, and post-award growth

### Why It Matters

NWMBA is designed to help minority-owned businesses secure contracts, manage growth, and strengthen access to industry relationships and business support.
