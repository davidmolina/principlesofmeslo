## SBA Surety Bond Guarantee Program

Bonding can be a gatekeeper for many contractors. The SBA Surety Bond Guarantee program helps contractors access surety bonds by guaranteeing a portion of the surety's loss if a contractor defaults.

### Why It Matters

If bonding capacity limits the size or type of projects you can bid, this is one of the first places to learn your options and requirements.
