## Go/No-Go Process

A structured decision-making step used to determine whether a project, bid, or opportunity is worth pursuing based on risk, capacity, profitability, and strategic alignment.

The role of the go/no-go process is to protect estimator time, improve pursuit quality, and ensure the team bids opportunities that fit company goals and capacity.
