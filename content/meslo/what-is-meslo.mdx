## What is MESLO

MESLO is a framework for structuring construction estimating and operational planning.

It stands for Material, Equipment, Subcontractors, Labor, and Other Costs.

The full MESLO structure also includes Insurance/Bond, Overhead, Profit, and Taxes.
Together, these nine dimensions support the final bid price.

By organizing estimates across these nine dimensions, contractors can bring discipline,
clarity, and defensibility to their pricing and execution.
