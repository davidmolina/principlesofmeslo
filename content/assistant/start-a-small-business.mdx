## How do you start a small business?

Start with a simple operating plan:

1. Define one core service and your ideal customer.
2. Set up your legal entity, bank account, and basic bookkeeping.
3. Build a clear offer with pricing, scope, and turnaround times.
4. Create a basic website with a booking/contact flow.
5. Start local outreach with fliers, referrals, and direct visits.
6. Track leads, proposals, wins, and customer feedback weekly.

For service businesses, speed and consistency matter most early. Keep the process simple, measurable, and repeatable.

## How should a small business promote services in the community?

- Visit apartment and multi-family properties within a 15-mile radius and leave fliers with property managers and leasing supervisors.
- Contact general contractors on active vertical projects that need final cleaning.
- Drop off fliers with small businesses within a 5-mile radius.
- Leave a magnet with a QR code at nearby residences to drive direct booking on your website.
