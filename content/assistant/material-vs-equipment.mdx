## What is the difference between material and equipment

Material is everything that stays at the jobsite permanently. If you are a painter that would be any, and all material expended onsite: the primer, paint, and anything else you installed on the jobsite, whether temporary or permanent. This is not to be confused with demolition, which falls in a different section altogether called other, otherwise known as ancillary costs and the "O" in MESLO.

Equipment, on the other hand, is everything you will need to perform the work, be it rented, leased, or owned (e.g., workvan, job trailer, commercial paint sprayers). Any piece of equipment required to perform the work goes in the equipment sections along with hourly/daily rates.
