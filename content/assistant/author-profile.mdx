## Meet The Author

DAVID MOLINA is an Mexican-American entrepreneur, founder and blogger. A son of immigrants from Nayarit, a former farm worker and high school drop-out, he went on to be the first in his family to attend and graduate from a university, earn an Officer Commission in Infantry through Army ROTC, and become addicted to servant leadership. Molina has founded, co-founded and launched a wide range of companies and organizations, advised companies and his work has been featured in multiple news outlets including The Bend Bulletin, Portland Business Journal, Univision KUNP-TV, Humans of Tech, and The Seattle Times.
