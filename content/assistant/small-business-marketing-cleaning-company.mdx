## Small business marketing

I'm a small business cleaning company-- what recommendations do you have to promote my services/ product in the community?

- Hit up all the apartment and multi family real estate complexes within a 15m radius and drop off fliers with the property manager/ leasing supervisor.
- Ensure all GCs going vertical— they need final clean.
- All small businesses within 5m radius hit them up with a flier.
- All residences within 5m radius leave a magnet with QR code to go to your website and book an appointment.
