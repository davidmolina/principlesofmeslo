## What all goes into the MESLO formula

Beautiful, transformative, and an elegant solution to what ails many businesses, the MESLO formula goal incorporates direct and indirect costs, as well as general and administrative (G&A) costs, to give us the value of x (bid price) and better understand the value of t (time).

The Principles of MESLO takes out the mystery and guess work in moving from receiving the bid to using a system and process to bid a well thought out number, quickly and efficiently.

It is a high-level formula designed to bring radical transparency to your estimate. It granularly job costs each major section. It incorporates risk factors. Separates your overhead, profit, and taxes. It helps in your decision-making process on whether you should bid this or not, increase or decrease your final price and if so, from where?
