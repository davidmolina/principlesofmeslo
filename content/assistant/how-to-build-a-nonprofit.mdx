## How to Build a Nonprofit

Source: [How To Build A Nonprofit (2023 Guide)](https://davidcmolina.com/2023/11/16/how-to-build-a-non-profit/)

Start with the problem first, then backward-plan the operating system of the organization.

Practical sequence:

1. Define the exact problem your nonprofit will solve.
2. Secure your domain name and social handles early.
3. Build core documents and filing readiness.
4. Recruit committed founding board members who can contribute time and/or money.
5. Coordinate legal and accounting setup for nonprofit filings.
6. Transition from founding board setup into governing board discipline.

The central principle is to begin with mission clarity and then align structure, filings, leadership, and operations around that mission.
