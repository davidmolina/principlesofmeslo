## How do you start a nonprofit?

Use a phased approach:

1. Define the mission, target population, and measurable impact goals.
2. Confirm legal structure and state/federal filing requirements.
3. Build a qualified board and define governance roles.
4. Create a first-year operating plan and budget.
5. Set up compliance, accounting, and donation tracking systems.
6. Launch with focused programs and a realistic fundraising strategy.

## What should be in a nonprofit launch plan?

- Mission statement and program scope
- Board structure and bylaws
- Budget, cash flow, and reporting cadence
- Compliance calendar and required filings
- Funding plan across grants, donors, and partnerships
- Communication plan for community, volunteers, and supporters

A nonprofit should be treated like an operating system, not only a legal filing. Clarity in governance and execution is what sustains long-term impact.
