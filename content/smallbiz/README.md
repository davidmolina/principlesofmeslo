# Small Business MDX

Place ordered small-business MDX knowledge entries here.

Examples:

- `001-recruiting-employees.mdx`
- `002-writing-a-job-description.mdx`
- `100-entrepreneurial-resilience.mdx`

Nested folders such as `hiring/`, `marketing/`, `sales/`, `finance/`, `operations/`, and `leadership/` are also supported by the retrieval loader.
